Privacy Policy: Arabic RTL for Slack

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The short version

This extension does not collect, store, send or sell any of your data. Nothing you read or type in Slack leaves your browser because of it.

What the extension does

It runs only on https://app.slack.com. It looks at the text of messages and the message box on the page so it can tell whether a line is Arabic, and then changes how that line is aligned on your screen. This all happens inside your browser. The extension has no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What it stores

One setting: whether the extension is switched on or off. Chrome keeps this with its own extension storage (chrome.storage.sync), which may sync it between your own Chrome browsers if you have Chrome sync turned on. It contains no message text and nothing that identifies you.

Permissions

  • Access to app.slack.com: needed to align Arabic text on Slack's pages. It is not used on any other site.
  • Storage: needed to remember the on/off setting.

Third parties

The extension shares no data with anyone. If the popup shows a "support this extension" link and you click it, that opens a separate website in a new tab, which has its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub.
